## Research Identity

I am an applied statistician developing statistical methodology for **complex systems**, where uncertainty, heterogeneity, and changing data-generating processes challenge conventional statistical assumptions.

My research spans applications in quantitative finance, economics, scientific computing, biomedical imaging, and other data-intensive domains. Rather than focusing on any single application area, I am interested in the methodological questions that arise across them.

> **How can statistical inference remain reliable when the assumptions underlying our models no longer hold?**

I study this question by combining ideas from **statistical modelling, econometrics, machine learning, and uncertainty quantification**. My work focuses on identifying hidden structure, validating statistical models under realistic conditions, and developing methods that remain robust when data are heterogeneous, nonstationary, partially observed, or affected by measurement uncertainty.

My long-term research agenda is centered on developing statistical methodology for

- **heterogeneous and complex data-generating processes**
- **robust statistical inference under uncertainty**
- **model validation and uncertainty quantification**
- **distribution shift and structural change**
- **statistical learning for scientific and quantitative applications**

### Hidden Regimes of Financial Instability
#### *A Finite Mixture Modelling Approach to Systemic Banking Crises*

<p>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Under%20Review-475569?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/R-Finite%20Mixture%20Models-0f172a?style=flat-square&logo=r&logoColor=white"/>
</p>

**Research Question**

Can systemic banking crises be explained by a single global model, or do they emerge from distinct latent macro-financial regimes?

**Contribution**

Developed a finite-mixture framework that identifies latent crisis regimes before estimating regime-specific econometric models. The results show that the importance of macro-financial predictors depends on the underlying regime, suggesting that pooled models can obscure fundamentally different crisis mechanisms.

**Keywords**

`Finite Mixture Models` `Panel Econometrics` `Latent Heterogeneity` `Expectation-Maximization` `Model Validation`

### The Illusion of Predictability
#### *Structural Heterogeneity and Informational Noise in Corporate Greenwashing*

<p>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Under%20Review-475569?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/R-Recursive%20Partitioning-0f172a?style=flat-square&logo=r&logoColor=white"/>
</p>

**Research Question**

When predictive performance is weak, does it reflect an inadequate statistical model or an intrinsic limit of the observable information?

**Contribution**

Combined global regression, recursive partitioning, and repeated out-of-sample validation to show that stronger local models do not necessarily improve overall prediction. The findings suggest that low predictive performance may itself be evidence of fragmented or unstable information rather than poor model specification.

**Keywords**

`Model-Based Recursive Partitioning` `Bootstrap Validation` `Predictive Modelling` `Model Risk` `Structural Heterogeneity`

### Trapped by Stability
#### *A Falsification Framework for Acquisition-Driven Radiomic Phenotypes*

<p>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-In%20Preparation-475569?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-Falsification%20Framework-0f172a?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white"/>
</p>

**Research Question**

Can stable patterns in radiomic data arise from image acquisition rather than underlying biological structure?

**Contribution**

Developing a falsification framework that evaluates whether radiomic phenotypes remain robust after accounting for acquisition protocols. The objective is to distinguish biologically meaningful structure from measurement-induced artifacts before downstream statistical modelling.

**Keywords**

`Radiomics` `Statistical Validation` `Measurement Bias` `High-Dimensional Data` `Scientific Machine Learning`

#### *Adaptive Workflow Orchestration for Large-Scale Scientific Computing*

**Research Question**

How can workflow orchestration systems make reliable scheduling decisions when runtime predictions, resource demand, and workload characteristics are uncertain and continuously evolving?
