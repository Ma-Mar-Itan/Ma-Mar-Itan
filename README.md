## About

I develop probabilistic and machine-learning frameworks to model **regime shifts**, **systemic instability**, and **extreme events** in complex adaptive systems — from financial markets to drone swarms.

The same structural question runs through all of it: *what latent structure governs observed behavior, and when does it break?*

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;🏦 &nbsp;Financial crisis modeling and systemic risk<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;🤖 &nbsp;Swarm intelligence and decentralized multi-agent systems<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;📊 &nbsp;Distribution shift, domain confounding, and transportability<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⚠️ &nbsp;Early-warning systems for latent instability<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;🔬 &nbsp;Statistical rigor in high-dimensional settings<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;✅ &nbsp;Reproducible, leakage-safe validation pipelines
