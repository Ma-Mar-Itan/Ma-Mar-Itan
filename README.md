## ◢ Positioning

I develop statistical and AI-assisted systems for understanding instability in complex adaptive systems.

The work focuses on the hidden structures beneath observed behavior: latent regimes, nonlinear dependencies, structural breaks, and the moments where the underlying **data-generating process** changes.

The central question is:

> **What mechanism is generating the system we observe — and when does that mechanism cease to apply?**

This perspective spans macro-financial systems, institutional dynamics, machine learning, and large-scale inference under uncertainty. The objective is not merely prediction, but the identification of the structures, constraints, and regime shifts that govern complex systems across changing environments.

From economic stress propagation to long-context reasoning systems, the goal is to build models that remain interpretable when the conditions that produced the data are themselves unstable.

### &nbsp;&nbsp;▸&nbsp; **Project Stratum** &nbsp;&nbsp;<sub><i>flagship · in development</i></sub>

A macro-financial **digital twin** for national resilience research. Stratum ingests econometric, institutional, and macro-prudential signals to model **nonlinear interdependencies** between fiscal, financial, and external sectors — producing interpretable what-if surfaces for policy stress-testing.

## &nbsp;◢&nbsp;&nbsp;Operating Philosophy

<table border="0">
<tr>
<td valign="top" width="3%"><b>I.</b></td>
<td><b>Structure precedes prediction.</b> Before forecasting a system, identify the latent regime generating its data. A model that ignores its own conditioning will fail at the moment it matters.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top" width="3%"><b>II.</b></td>
<td><b>Instability is informative.</b> Crises are not noise around equilibrium — they are the only moments where the true topology of a system becomes legible.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top" width="3%"><b>III.</b></td>
<td><b>Interpretability is non-optional.</b> A model whose mechanism cannot be audited cannot inform policy, allocate sovereign capital, or be trusted under adversarial conditions.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top" width="3%"><b>IV.</b></td>
<td><b>Distribution shift is the default.</b> The world that produced the training data is not the world the model will operate in. Transportability is the first-class problem, not an afterthought.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top" width="3%"><b>V.</b></td>
<td><b>Sovereignty is computational.</b> The capacity to model one's own institutional, fiscal, and informational topology is no longer optional infrastructure — it is the precondition for strategic autonomy.</td>
</tr>
</table>
